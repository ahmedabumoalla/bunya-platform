# Invoice sales policy

Updated 2026-11-09 (Asia/Riyadh). Applies to the invoice presentation, separately from the quotation policy in `price-quotation-policy.ts`.

The owner requested seller protection, inspection and acknowledgement on delivery, and restrictions on returns. The invoice wording defines supply scope, purchaser handling responsibilities, documented receipt, returns, defects and statutory rights. It does not claim government approval or guarantee freedom from disputes. This is an engineering implementation of a researched draft, not a Saudi lawyer's legal opinion; qualified local counsel should review it against the actual sale channels, goods and contracts.

## Official sources checked

- [Ministry of Commerce: prohibition of blanket no-return statements](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/news_1833.aspx). A general prohibition on returns cannot remove remedies for defective, adulterated or nonconforming goods. The invoice therefore limits *additional discretionary* returns while preserving mandatory remedies.
- [E-Commerce Law, article 13](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/360de590-0286-4fa5-a243-aa9100c31979/1). Where applicable, the consumer's seven-day cancellation right for unused products is preserved, with the law's costs and exceptions. The custom-manufacture exception is limited to products made to the customer's order/specification, and does not eliminate defect/nonconformity rights. The law page was also checked through official indexed text and the [Ministry's explanation](https://mc.gov.sa/ar/mediacenter/News/Pages/09-07-19-02.aspx); direct requests intermittently timed out.
- [Civil Transactions Law, articles 338–344](https://laws.boe.gov.sa/BoeLaws/Laws/LawDetails/655fdb42-8c96-422b-b8c4-b04f0095c94c/1). The policy distinguishes observable condition at receipt from latent defects, requires reasonable notification and preserves the seller's legal obligations. It does not impose an invented 24-hour forfeiture deadline or substitute manufacturer warranty for seller liability.

## Operational boundaries

- An invoice is not evidence that delivery occurred. Actual recipient identity, authority, date, signature and exceptions belong on a delivery record; this change does not fabricate or collect that acknowledgement.
- Terms should be disclosed and agreed before contracting where agreement is required. Printing a new policy on an existing invoice does not retroactively amend the contract or establish acceptance.
- Financial snapshots, tax calculations, invoice numbers, QR content and issuance gates are unchanged by policy wording. The policy version is a source identifier, not a claim of persisted customer consent.
- An invoice printing change does not implement refund processing, credit notes, delivery signing or new consumer claims workflows.
